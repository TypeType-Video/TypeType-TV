Twitch on TV.

- Watch Twitch: lives, past broadcasts and clips, in a Live tab with the lives you follow, the past broadcasts of the
  channels you follow, the popular lives and the lives of the biggest categories.
- Open Twitch channel pages, search Twitch channels, lives and categories, and browse the lives of a category.
- Read the Twitch chat beside a live, with Twitch, BTTV, FFZ and 7TV emotes, badges and the colors of names.
- Start a Twitch live over from its beginning, go back to the live at any time, and see the game chapters of past
  broadcasts.
- Connect your Twitch account in Settings > Twitch with a QR code or a code, and follow channels.
- Show the recommendation rows of your instance on the home screen.
- Choose when a video counts as watched in Settings > Playback.
- Resume a YouTube video when YouTube asks the instance to renew its session, instead of giving up after a few seconds.

Twitch needs server 1.18 with Twitch turned on. Resuming after a session renewal needs server 1.19.0-rc.2 or newer.

Download `TypeType-TV-v0.7.0.apk` below; the `.sha256` file lets you check the download.

Betas are published as prereleases. A beta installs over the stable release, and the next stable release installs
over the beta.
