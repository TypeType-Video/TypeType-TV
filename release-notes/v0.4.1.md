A clearer Subscriptions tab and an easier search filter.

- Subscriptions: the channels you follow are now a row of avatars at the top, with "All" first. OK on a channel
  shows only its videos; the videos below are larger, three per row.
- Search: the kind of result (videos, playlists, channels, music) is now chosen from tabs above the keyboard and
  the results. The old wheel opened over the keyboard and hid some keys.

Download `TypeType-TV-v0.4.1.apk` below; the `.sha256` file lets you check the download.

Betas are published as prereleases. A beta installs over the stable release, and the next stable release installs
over the beta.
