Creator end screens on TV.

- Player: the creator's end screens show over the last seconds of a YouTube video. Press Up to reach the cards, OK to
  open the video, playlist or channel, and Back to return to the video. You can hide them in Settings > Content.
- Update the libraries the app is built with.

End screens need a server from 1.16.2-rc.7 on.

Download `TypeType-TV-v0.6.0.apk` below; the `.sha256` file lets you check the download.

Betas are published as prereleases. A beta installs over the stable release, and the next stable release installs
over the beta.
