Sign in to TypeType TV from your phone.

- The sign-in screen now opens on a QR code. Scan it with your phone, sign in to your instance there if needed,
  and confirm: the TV signs in without typing anything with the remote.
- You can also open the address shown under the code on your phone and type the code by hand.
- Use a password switches back to the usual form. On instances that do not offer QR sign-in yet, or where the
  admin turned it off, the TV shows the password form directly.
- The startup intro stays smooth while the first screen loads.

QR sign-in needs TypeType 1.13.0 or later on the instance.

Download `TypeType-TV-v0.3.0.apk` below; the `.sha256` file lets you check the download.

Betas are published as prereleases. A beta installs over the stable release, and the next stable release installs
over the beta.
