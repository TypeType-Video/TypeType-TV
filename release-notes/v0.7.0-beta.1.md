TypeType TV 0.7.0-beta.1

- Watch Twitch: lives, past broadcasts and clips, with a Live on Twitch row on the home screen.
- Open Twitch channel pages with their videos and clips.
- Search Twitch channels, lives and categories, and browse the lives of a category.
- Read the Twitch chat beside a live.
- Start a Twitch live over from its beginning, and go back to the live at any time.
- See the game chapters of Twitch past broadcasts.
- Connect your Twitch account in Settings > Twitch with a QR code or a code, follow channels, and find the channels you follow on the home screen.
- Show the recommendation rows of your instance on the home screen.
- Choose when a video counts as watched in Settings > Playback.
- Say when a Twitch stream has ended or a scheduled live has not started yet.

Twitch needs server 1.18 with Twitch turned on. The watched setting needs a server newer than 1.18.0-rc.7.

I tested this beta against servers 1.18.0-rc.7 and 1.18.0-beta.357. I could not test connecting a Twitch account to the end, or following channels with one, so tell me if something goes wrong there.
