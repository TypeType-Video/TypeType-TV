TypeType TV 0.7.0-beta.2

- See Twitch, BTTV, FFZ and 7TV emotes, badges and the colors of names in the chat beside a live.
- Add a Live tab with the lives you follow, the past broadcasts of the channels you follow, the popular lives and the lives of the biggest categories.
- Resume a YouTube video when YouTube asks the instance to renew its session, instead of giving up after a few seconds.

Twitch needs server 1.18 with Twitch turned on. Resuming in that case needs server 1.19.0-rc.2 or newer.

I tested this beta on an Android TV 16 emulator against server 1.19.0-rc.2.
