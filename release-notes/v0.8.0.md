TypeType TV 0.8.0

- Choose the services your home shows from the menu under your profile picture: YouTube, Bilibili, NicoNico, one of
  them or a mix. The choice stays on the TV.
- Start the home faster: it loads its first screen first and the rows below as you scroll. The Twitch rows moved to
  the Live tab, which now loads its categories as you scroll and comes back to the live you opened.
- Choose your preferred video codec in Settings > Playback: Automatic, AV1, VP9 or H.264.
- Add a screensaver in Settings > Screensaver: Tyme, the thumbnails of your home or the TypeType logo, darkened as you
  like down to a black screen for falling asleep with music on. Start now shows it right away.
- Skip sponsor segments right on time, skip back-to-back segments in one go, and let a segment at the very end finish
  the video.
- Stop videos from reloading near their end, or when the server answers errors for a few seconds.
- Go back in one press on Android 13 to 15.
- Install updates already optimized for your TV, so the first start after an update is smoother.

Twitch needs server 1.18 with Twitch turned on.
