First public release of TypeType TV, the Android TV client for TypeType.

TypeType TV needs a TypeType Server instance: the server chosen during setup handles extraction, playback sessions,
recommendations, and synchronization. The app runs on Android TV, Google TV, and Fire TV devices with Android 6.0 or
newer, and does not require Google Play Services. It supports YouTube, Bilibili, and NicoNico.

## Highlights

- Home feed, search, channels, playlists, and a library with history, favorites, and Watch Later.
- Playback up to 4K with AVC, VP9, AV1, or HEVC, chosen from the decoders of the device, and HDR on HDR screens.
  When a decoder fails, the app remembers it and plays the next videos in another codec.
- Quality, audio language, captions, chapters, SponsorBlock, comments, the description, and the channel, all
  reachable from the player without stopping the video.
- YouTube live streams with their chat beside the picture.
- A Performance section in the settings for slower devices: animations, previews, the background picture, and a
  shorter or silent startup intro.

## Install

Download `TypeType-TV-v0.1.0.apk` below and install it on the TV, for example with a file manager or `adb install`.
The `.sha256` file lets you check the download.

Betas are published as prereleases. A beta installs over the stable release, and the next stable release installs
over the beta.
